Dear Students Please be Familiar with the rules of election

The following are the rules of MUSTSO leadership election

  1. Voter Eligibility:You must be a officially registered student of the college for the current accademic yearwith an active account in the system.
  2. Vote Secrecy:Your vote is your secret. It is strictly forbidden to show,share or cast your vote under the influence or pessure of anyone else.
  3. Voting Limit:Each student is allowed to vote only once. Once you submit your vote your account will be locked automatically, and you can not change your choice.
    Please choose wisely and made correct choice.
  4. Account Security:It is a serious disciplinary offense to use another student's registration number or password. Strict legal and disciplinary actions will be taken against anyone attempting unauthorized access.
  5. Constituency scope:The system will present candidates for the president, your specific college MP, and your specific department MP based on your registration details.
    You must cast a vote in all category before submiting.
  6. Voting Deadline:All votes must be cast before the specified deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  7. Technical issues:If a system error or Network failure occurs before completion,The votter must report immedietly to the electroral committee.
    A vote is only counted if you receive the final "Success Message".
  8. Acceptance of Terms:By checking the box and clicking "proceed", you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms and conditions.
